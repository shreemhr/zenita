Why spend Thanksgiving at Zenita

A holiday meal worth the climb

Zenita sits atop the historic Aztec Hotel on the San Antonio River Walk, a rooftop made for moments of energy and inspiration in the sky. This Thanksgiving, we're setting the table up here for you.

01

Familiar, then surprising

Turkey, stuffing, mashed potatoes, gravy and pumpkin, all there. Then the twist: bright kimchi, savory miso and silky bok choy that make every bite feel new without losing the comfort.

02

A table with a skyline

Trade the crowded dining room for an open sky over downtown San Antonio. Come for a sunlit Thanksgiving lunch, book an early dinner to catch golden hour, or a later one when the city lights come up below you.

03

Zero cooking. Zero dishes.

No shopping list, no 6 am oven timer, no sink full of pans at midnight. Just three courses served to your table and more time with the people you came to celebrate.

The Thanksgiving menu

A one-day-only menu from the Zenita kitchen. Add the wine pairing and we'll pour a glass chosen to match each course.

Thanksgiving at Zenita

First course

Kimchi Caesar Salad

Crisp romaine tossed in our house kimchi Caesar dressing, with tempura anchovies, Grana Padano and crispy wonton strips. A Zenita favorite with a little heat to wake up the appetite.

Main course

Turkey Roulade

with Kimchi, Apple, Celery, Carrot & Breadcrumb Stuffing

Tender rolled turkey wrapped around a stuffing that's sweet, savory and gently tangy. Served with Miso Mashed Potatoes, Braised Bok Choy and Turkey Gravy.

Dessert

Pumpkin Cheesecake Crème Brûlée

Two holiday classics in one: creamy pumpkin cheesecake custard under a crackling caramelized-sugar top.

Good to know

Before you book

When is it offered?

Thanksgiving Day only: Thursday, November 26, 2026. Lunch seatings run every 15 minutes from 11:00 am to 2:00 pm, and dinner seatings from 5:00 to 10:00 pm. The same three-course menu and prices apply to both.

How does payment work?

Prices are per person and paid in full when you book on OpenTable.

Can I bring a big group?

Yes. Reservations are available for 1 to 20 guests, so the whole family can sit together.

Allergies or dietary needs?

Please note any allergies in your reservation so our kitchen can prepare for your visit.

What should I wear?

Come as you are for the holiday. Late-November evenings on the rooftop can turn cool, so a light layer is a good idea for dinner.
