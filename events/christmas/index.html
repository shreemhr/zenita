Why Christmas at Zenita

The holiday table, set in the sky

Make this the Christmas everyone remembers, and the first one in years where the host gets to sit down too.

i.

Christmas above the city

Ascend to the rooftop of the historic Aztec Hotel and dine inside or out on the terrace, with downtown San Antonio and the River Walk below.

ii.

San Antonio flavor in every course

Our chefs put a local twist on the classic holiday roast: Abuelita chocolate and chili in the rub, mole negro in the demi-glace. A Nochebuena dinner you won't find anywhere else.

iii.

A night off for the whole family

No shopping list, no hours in the kitchen and no sink full of dishes. Just bring the people you love. Tables for 1 to 20, so the whole family fits.
