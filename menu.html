Aztec Carajillo

18

A coffee drink from Spain and Latin America that is traditionally made with Liquor 43. Ours also features Brugal rum and is served tableside upon request.

Caipirinha

14

A Brazilian cocktail made with muddled lime, sugar, and cachaça — a distilled spirit made from fermented sugarcane juice, similar to rum.

Cantarito

16

A tequila-based cocktail featuring orange juice, lime juice, and grapefruit juice. Traditionally served in a clay pot known as a jarrito de barro that helps keep the drink cold, and rimmed with Tajín.

Cherry Blossom Paloma

14

Our paloma is made with tequila, lime, and grapefruit soda and is considered by many to be Mexico's national drink. In our version, we replace the lime with Yuzu liqueur & cherry blossom syrup.

Mango Mojito

14

Light rum with muddled mint, lime, & mango. A tropical twist on this Caribbean favorite.

Prickly Pear Yuzu Margarita

16

The nopal, or prickly pear cactus, was considered sacred by the Aztecs and consumed medicinally. We combine Yuzu liqueur, silver tequila, prickly pear puree, finished with a dash of agave nectar for balance.

Spicy Cucumber Martini

16

Classic muddled cucumber, fresh squeezed lime juice and Hendricks Gin, elevated with house-made jalapeno simple syrup and a Tajín rim.

Strawberry Pisco Sour

15

A fruity and floral blend of Peruvian pisco, a grape-based brandy spirit, with lemon juice and fresh strawberries.

Suntory Toki Black Old Fashioned

16

Suntory Toki Black provides aromas of baked apple & spice, while Aztec chocolate bitters and charred jalapeno combine their heat in our version of the timeless cocktail.

Zenita Espresso Martini

14

Features vanilla-infused tequila, coffee-infused tequila, and our house-made cinnamon simple syrup shaken with espresso.
